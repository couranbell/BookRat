About BookRat

We are a relatively young and independent bookstore in London, with a strong ambition to unite people in these challenging times. Alongside our community events, we offer a wide collection of books: from romance and business to crime fiction and poetry, from memoirs to classic literature, and more. So you can be sure that you won't leave empty-handed.

However, dropping by just to see what's happening and have a chat is always welcomme. We regularly host book presentations, author meetings, and book club events, so you can join our community and make reading a meaningful part of your life! And that is exactly our aim!
